Customer Segmentation and Retention Campaign ROI

Online Retail II · Python · Pandas · Power BI
GitHub repo ↗
5,852customers segmented into 6 groups
11.1highest ROI, At Risk segment
70%of revenue from 24% of customers
Situation

A retailer's marketing team had a limited retention budget and no data-backed way to choose which of its 5,852 customers (£16.77M revenue) to target.

Task

Find the segment with the highest retention-campaign ROI and recommend how to split the budget.

Action

Cleaned the data (dropped 243,007 rows with no Customer ID), scored Recency and Frequency 1 to 5, built an ROI and budget-allocation model in Python, and a 5-page Power BI dashboard with What-if sliders for margin, budget and hurdle ROI.

Result

A £5,000 budget splits into £2,121 for At Risk and £2,879 for Champions, for about £44.5K expected net profit. Champions overtake At Risk above 2.9% response, so I recommend a holdout test before scaling.

Situation

A candy distributor needed to understand profitability across 15 product SKUs and 5 manufacturing facilities.

Task

Find which products drive profit, where margin is weak and where supply risk sits.

Ran Pareto analysis on order data, mapped factory dependencies, and delivered a research paper, an executive summary and an interactive dashboard.

System Capacity and Care Load Analytics

CBP/HHS Unaccompanied Children Program · Python · Pandas · Streamlit
GitHub repo ↗
1,075days of federal data (2023 to 2025)
86 → 4false-positive anomalies
26prolonged system-strain windows found
Situation

Federal program data over 1,075 days had unreported days and noisy anomaly flags, which made capacity monitoring unreliable.

Task

Build a trustworthy pipeline and dashboard to track system capacity and care load.

Action

Built a gap-corrected Python/Pandas pipeline with 5 capacity metrics and 5 KPIs, excluded 355 unreported days with an is_reported flag, added a backlog-adjusted validation check, and deployed a live Streamlit dashboard.

Result

Cut false-positive anomalies from 86 to 4 and surfaced 5 multi-day reporting gaps and 26 prolonged system-strain windows. The dashboard is public on Streamlit Community Cloud.
